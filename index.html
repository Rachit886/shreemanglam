About Us

A family business built on trust

For over three decades, we have been supplying quality plywood and hardware to carpenters, interior designers, contractors and homeowners. We stock only genuine, branded material so your furniture and interiors last for years.

Whether it is a small repair or a full home project, our team helps you pick the right material at the right price, with honest billing. Visit our shop and check every product with your own eyes before you buy.
